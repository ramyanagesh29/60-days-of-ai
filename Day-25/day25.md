# Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator 🦈

## ABTalks 60-Day Claude Challenge

### Project Overview

For Day 25, I built an AI Shark Tank Simulator using Claude.

The application allows a founder to enter a startup idea and experience a simulated investor pitch session with four AI judges, each evaluating the startup from a different perspective.

## Features

- Startup pitch input
- Four AI investor perspectives:
  - Venture Capitalist – Market size & scalability
  - Founder – Execution
  - Customer Advocate – Real-world usefulness
  - Angel Investor – Profitability & unit economics
- Investor Q&A round
- Dynamic judge reactions
- Startup scorecard
- Investment decision
- Suggested valuation
- Funding recommendation
- Pitch report PDF download
- Leaderboard
- Share result option
- Confetti for successful outcomes
- Responsive dark Shark Tank-inspired interface

## Evaluation Criteria

The startup is evaluated on:

- Market Potential
- Innovation
- Business Model
- Execution
- Investment Worthiness
