Small habits that compound — literally.

Money Tips for Students
01Follow the 50/30/20 idea loosely — needs, wants, savings — even on a small allowance. Even ₹50/week saved builds a habit.
02Track every expense for one week without judging yourself. Awareness comes before control.
03Separate "wants" from "needs" before you buy. Wait 24 hours on anything over 20% of your weekly allowance.
04Use student discounts everywhere — GitHub Student Pack, Canva Pro, transit passes, food apps.
05Keep a small emergency buffer separate from your goals — even ₹200 avoids a crisis borrow.
06Round up: if lunch costs ₹47, note it as ₹50 and stash the difference. Small rounding adds up over a semester.
