Day 24 — Business Strategy with Claude
ABTalks 60-Day Claude Challenge
🎯 Task

Turn a startup idea into a sustainable business model by using Claude as an AI Co-Founder, Growth Strategist, YC Advisor, and Business Consultant — evaluating it strictly on revenue, distribution, and evidence rather than features or vanity metrics.

📥 Input

Customer_MVP_Blueprint.pdf — the Day 23 output, a Customer & MVP Blueprint for AI Learning Buddy for Bharat, a vernacular-first AI tutoring product for tier-2/3 Indian students and parents.

🧠 Prompt

INPUT

Use the Day 23 Blueprint as the source of truth. First summarize the startup in 5-10 bullets, then extract customer, MVP, value proposition, pricing, revenue, and GTM assumptions.

TASK

Determine whether this startup can become a sustainable business.

Focus on:

Revenue over vanity metrics
Distribution over features
Evidence over assumptions
Practical execution over theory
ANALYSIS
1. Business Reality Check
Who pays?
Why do they pay?
How will they discover the product?
Biggest growth risks?
Biggest monetization risks?
Weak assumptions to validate?
2. Business Strategy Report

Provide:

Executive Summary
Business Model Canvas
Revenue & Pricing Strategy
Go-To-Market Strategy
Customer Acquisition Strategy
First 100 Users Plan
Competitive Position & Moat
Reverse SWOT Analysis
Investor One-Liner
30-Second Founder Pitch
Founder Action Sheet (Top 10 Actions)
3. Investment Scorecard (0-100)

Score:

Business Viability
Revenue Potential
GTM Strength
Competitive Strength
Investor Readiness

Include brief reasoning.

4. Visual Dashboard

Create a single HTML/SVG infographic showing:

Business Model
Revenue Streams
GTM Flow
First 100 Users Plan
Competitive Moat
Key Risks
Score Cards
Final Verdict

Verdict: 🟢 Investable 🟡 Validate 🟠 Pivot 🔴 Reject

OUTPUT

Generate a professional PDF-ready report with:

Title Page
Table of Contents
Executive Summary
Business Strategy Report
Visual Dashboard
Founder Action Sheet

📦 What Claude Generated
Startup Summary & Extracted Assumptions — customer, MVP, value prop, pricing, revenue, and GTM assumptions pulled from the Day 23 blueprint
Business Reality Check — who pays, why, discovery channels, biggest growth/monetization risks, weakest assumptions to validate
Business Strategy Report
Business Model Canvas
Revenue & Pricing Strategy
Go-To-Market Strategy
Customer Acquisition Strategy
First 100 Users Plan
Competitive Position & Moat
Reverse SWOT Analysis
Investor One-Liner + 30-Second Founder Pitch
Investment Scorecard (0–100) — Business Viability, Revenue Potential, GTM Strength, Competitive Strength, Investor Readiness
Visual Dashboard — single-page infographic summarizing business model, revenue streams, GTM flow, first 100 users plan, competitive moat, key risks, scorecards, and final verdict
Founder Action Sheet — Top 10 next actions
🏁 Final Verdict

🟡 VALIDATE — A real, underserved problem with strong founder-market fit, but no evidence yet of willingness to pay. The next 30 days should be a structured validation sprint (interviews → landing page → paid pilot → decide) before any further product investment or fundraising conversation.
