# Day 45 — AI Decision Strategist: Placement Prep Planning

## 🎯 Overview
An interactive HTML "Decision Report" built by turning Claude into an impartial Decision Strategist. Instead of asking Claude to just generate a study plan, I had it interview me first (4 structured questions) to surface the *real* decision underneath my "I don't have a plan" problem — then generate a full data-backed report.

## 🧩 The Problem
Stuck on how to prep for October on-campus drives while juggling:
- DSA preparation
- Communication skills
- Aptitude/Quant
- Placement-ready projects
- 7th semester academics
- 10–15 hrs/week lost to travel

## 🛠️ What Claude Built
A single self-contained interactive HTML dashboard (no external dependencies) including:
- **Real Decision Framing** — the actual trade-off, not the surface question
- **Case-for-each-option cards** — Sequential Focus vs Weekly Rotation vs Minimum Viable Prep
- **Assumption Buster** — named biases (overwhelm paralysis, all-or-nothing thinking) and blind spots
- **Decision Matrix** — 3 options scored across 7 weighted dimensions with animated bar charts
- **Premortem analysis** — for the top 2 options, imagining failure 12 months out
- **7-Day Test Plan** — a small validation sprint before committing 2-3 months
- **Verdict** — a decisive, defended recommendation
- **Shareable summary cards** — built for social screenshots

## 📊 Outcome
**Verdict: Weekly Rotation** — fixed weekly slots across DSA, communication, quant, and projects starting immediately, validated via a 7-day test plan before full commitment.

## 🧠 Key Takeaway
"Having a plan" doesn't mean covering everything equally — it means deciding what *not* to do yet. The interview-first approach (collecting answers before analyzing) forced clarity that a straight "make me a study plan" prompt wouldn't have surfaced.

## 🔗 Tech
- Single-file HTML/CSS/JS, dark navy/purple theme
- CSS-only animated matrix bars
- Fully responsive, no external JS dependencies
