The Real Decision

You're not choosing between two options — you're choosing how to sequence and split limited hours across DSA, communication, quant, and projects before October drives, while still handling 7th sem academics and 10-15 hrs/week lost to travel.

The hard part isn't knowing what to study. It's that everything feels equally urgent, so nothing gets a real block of time — and that's what's driving the overlap and anxiety.

The Case For Each Approach

Option A — Sequential Focus (deep-dive one area at a time)

Strongest Case

You get real depth fast — e.g. 3 weeks of pure DSA can move you from weak to interview-ready, since there's no context-switching tax.

Hidden Upside

Momentum and visible progress in one area builds confidence you carry into the next — less mental overlap, more "I've actually finished something."

Biggest Weakness

Whatever you sequence last (likely communication or projects) gets rushed right before drives — the worst time to under-prepare it.

Best if you value

Depth, focus, and finishing things — not doing five things at 60%.

Option B — Weekly Rotation (all 4 areas, fixed slots every week)

Strongest Case

Every area gets consistent touch-time from week 1, so nothing is "started too late" by October.

Hidden Upside

Communication and quant — the areas people neglect till the last month — actually get built as a habit, not a panic cram.

Biggest Weakness

With only 2-3 hrs/day free, thin slices across 4 areas can feel slow — you may not "finish" any one thing until close to October.

Best if you value

Steady, balanced readiness over deep expertise in one area by test time.

Option C — Minimum Viable Prep (light touch on all, cut scope hard)

Strongest Case

Realistic given travel + academics — you protect sleep and sem performance while still covering drive basics.

Hidden Upside

Forces you to identify the actual minimum bar for on-campus drives instead of over-preparing for things that don't matter yet.

Biggest Weakness

Risk of being "just okay" everywhere in a pool of students who went deep in at least one area.

Best if you value

Sustainability and protecting your CGPA over maximizing placement odds.

Assumption Buster

Assumptions you may be making

  • That "having a plan" means covering everything equally — it usually means deciding what NOT to do yet.
  • That travel time is fully lost time — some of it can carry audio-based prep (aptitude formulas, spoken English practice, DSA concept explainers).
  • That 2-3 months means you're starting from zero — you're not; existing MERN projects and current CGPA already give you a base to build on, not a blank slate.

Biases at play

What you're definitely ignoring: you haven't written down actual weekly hours available — "no plan" partly means you've never done the math on 2-3 hrs/day × 5 days minus travel minus academics. Until that number exists, any plan will feel like guessing.

Early warning sign: you're 5+ weeks in and haven't touched communication or quant at all.

Prevention action: hard-cap each sequential block at 3 weeks max, no exceptions, before rotating.

Option B (Rotation) failed because...

  • Daily slices were too thin to build real DSA problem-solving speed.
  • Weeks got "rebalanced" reactively instead of following the fixed slots.
  • No single area reached interview-ready level by October.

Early warning sign: you can't recall solving a genuinely hard DSA problem in the last 2 weeks — only easy/medium repeats.

Prevention action: track weekly "difficulty level reached" per area, not just hours logged.

The Verdict

Option B — Weekly Rotation

It wins because your real fear isn't any single subject — it's overlap and losing the timeline. Rotation guarantees every area gets built weekly, so nothing gets discovered "too late" right before drives.

The hard truth: rotation only works if you actually write down fixed weekly slots today — "spreading attention" without a written schedule is just a softer version of the overlap you're already scared of.

What could flip it: if the Day 7 test shows thin daily slices genuinely can't move your DSA level — then shift to a hybrid: sequential DSA block first, rotation for the rest.
