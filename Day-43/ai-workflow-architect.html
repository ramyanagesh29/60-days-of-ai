Workflow summary

What this pipeline gets you

A single feature — scoped, designed, built on both ends, tested, deployed, and watched — using AI at every stage to compress research, boilerplate, and debugging time, while keeping architecture and product decisions in your hands.

Where humans stay in the loop

  • Scoping the feature and saying no to scope creep
  • Reviewing every AI-generated diff before merge
  • Security-sensitive code: auth, payments, PII handling
  • Final go/no-go on production deploys
